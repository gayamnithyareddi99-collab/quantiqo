Do I need cryptographic expertise to use Quantiqo?

+
No. Quantiqo is specifically designed for finance managers, operations leads, and project managers without quantum cryptography training. The workflow-guided interface with PQC-ID assignment, automated RAID-Q logging, and compliance templates means you can build a professional-grade quantum-safe settlement rail without cryptographic expertise. How is Quantiqo different from Post-Quantum (UK), Arqit, or SAP?

+
Post-Quantum focuses on VPN and communication security. Arqit focuses on symmetric key agreement for networks. SAP handles human-centric payroll it cannot reconcile AI agentic output or score cryptographic debt. Quantiqo operates in an entirely different category: we assign billable PQC identities to AI agents, trigger micro-settlements upon verified task completion, and generate autonomous RAID-Q governance logs all wrapped in NIST-standard lattice cryptography. We are the operating system for the agentic economy that sits between ERP software and national quantum compliance.

What is the HNDL threat and how does Quantiqo defend against it?

+
Harvest Now, Decrypt Later (HNDL) is a state-level attack where encrypted financial and project data is intercepted today and stored until cryptographically relevant quantum computers (CRQCs) can decrypt it potentially in 5–10 years. For the UK's £11 trillion financial sector, today's pension records, defence contracts, and investment strategies are already a ticking liability. Quantiqo wraps every settlement record and RAID-Q log in NIST ML-DSA lattice signatures, ensuring that even future quantum computers cannot decrypt the data. This is not theoretical NCSC has issued formal guidance on HNDL risks in 2025.

How does the Crypto-Agility Engine reduce future compliance risk?

+
As NIST and NCSC update PQC standards over the 2025–2035 migration window, some algorithms may be deprecated or superseded. Quantiqo's Crypto-Agile architecture allows the platform to swap cryptographic primitives replacing one lattice algorithm with another without breaking live financial workflows, disrupting settlement rails, or requiring client-side re-deployment. This means your institution never faces emergency algorithm migration under regulatory pressure. The compliance engine monitors NCSC, PRA, and Bank of England directives in real time and updates operational boundaries automatically.
